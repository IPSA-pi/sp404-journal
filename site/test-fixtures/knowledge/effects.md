## Bus FX basics
- The effect buttons around the screen toggle the effects on BUS 1 and BUS 2.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-13, entry: 2026-10-13-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/index.html , status: manual -->
- `[REMAIN]` + `[BUS FX]` swaps the effects of BUS 1 and BUS 2; the parameter values are kept.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-13, entry: 2026-10-13-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/8004340378585483.html, status: manual -->
- `[SHIFT]` + `[BUS FX]` mutes the bus so only the effect sound is heard (MUTE BUS).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-13, entry: 2026-10-13-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: verified -->

## Delay
- Turning the delay off with its button cuts the feedback tail immediately; the flamingo echoes stop.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-13, entry: 2026-10-13-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: unverified -->
- Hold `[VALUE]` and press an effect button (EFFECT GRAB) to apply an effect only while held.
