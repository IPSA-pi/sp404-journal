## Question
What's the best way to sample from my turntable, and should I quantize patterns?

## Key takeaways
- Turn on the metronome with `[SHIFT]` + pad `[9]` before recording.
- Add a count-in with `[SHIFT]` + pad `[10]`.
- Set the input gain under `[SHIFT]` + pad `[12]` so the peaks stay below clipping.

## Details
Record a little longer than needed and trim with [START/END] afterwards.
