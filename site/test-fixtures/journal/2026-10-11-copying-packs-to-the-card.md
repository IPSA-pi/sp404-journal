## Question
Can the sampler import the FLAC files from my Bandcamp purchase?

## Key takeaways
- The unit imports WAV, AIFF and MP3 from `F:\IMPORT\`.
- Open the import screen with `[SHIFT]` + pad `[14]`.

## Details
FLAC needs converting to 48 kHz 16-bit WAV first, for example with ffmpeg.
