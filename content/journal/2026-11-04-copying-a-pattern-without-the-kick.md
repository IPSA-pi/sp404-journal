## Question
I have a pattern I'd like to copy and then remove the kick drum samples from. How?

## Key takeaways
- `[COPY]`, the source pattern pad, the destination pad, then `[REMAIN]`: the pads the pattern uses light dimly white. ([Extracting specific samples (pads) from a pattern](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7932916378504075.html))
- Press every pad to keep (all but the kick) so they light fully white, press `[REMAIN]` again, then `[VALUE]` or `[COPY]`. ([Extracting specific samples (pads) from a pattern](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7932916378504075.html))
- The copy overwrites the pattern on the destination pad; the source pattern is unchanged. ([Copying the pattern of a pad](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7932327578503307.html))
- Alternative: copy the whole pattern, open it in TR-REC (`[SHIFT]` + `[REC]` during playback), select the kick with `[SUB PAD]` + pad and press `[DEL]` + `[A/F]` for each measure. ([Creating a new pattern (TR-REC)](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7921805978489483.html))

## Details
The extract method does it in one step. To put the copy in another bank, press
that bank's button before the destination pad; `[CTRL 3]` picks another project.
Start from pattern mode (`[PATTERN SELECT]` lit) so the pads mean patterns; the
manual doesn't spell this out.

The TR-REC route works too, but `[DEL]` + `[A/F]` only clears one measure at a
time, so longer patterns need it repeated per measure (`[VALUE]` selects the measure).
