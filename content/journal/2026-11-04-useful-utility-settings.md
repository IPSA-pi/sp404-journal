## Question
What are some other good-to-know settings in the UTILITY menu?

## Key takeaways
- SYSTEM > GENERAL > Load Project: "Last" opens the last-used project at power-on, or pick a fixed project 1–16. ([SYSTEM](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html))
- Sub Pad Mode: `[SUB PAD]` retriggers the current pad (Retrig) or switches to skip-back (SkipBack). ([SYSTEM](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html))
- PTN Change Mode: samples stop when the pattern changes (MKII) or keep playing (SX). Bank Mute ON mutes a sample when one in another bank plays. ([SYSTEM](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html))
- Pop-up Time (Normal, Short, OFF) and Start-up Screen "Skip" cut screen clutter. ([SYSTEM](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html))
- GAIN > Noise Gate (OFF, -9, -12, -18 dB, -Inf) reduces the noise floor on LINE IN and INPUT. ([SYSTEM](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html))
- PAD SET > TRIGGER: Curve Type (Lin, Exp, Log, Fix), Threshold against vibration triggers, Trig Span against double hits. ([PAD SETTING](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7971073178546315.html))

## Details
The UTILITY menu (`[SHIFT]` + pad `[13]`) holds SYSTEM (tabs GENERAL, CLICK,
MIDI, GAIN, VERSION), PAD SET, EFX SET, RENAME, import/export, BACKUP/RESTORE
and FACTORY RESET. Also covered in the answer, and already in the knowledge base:
Edit/EFX Knob Mode, MARK Function, Reverse Type, BPM Auto Dtct, the CLICK
count-in and metronome settings, MIDI Sync, output gains, LED and pad colours,
FAVORITE, DIRECT FX and SIDE CHAIN.

Making a backup before bulk deletes (like deleting a bank) is a good habit.
