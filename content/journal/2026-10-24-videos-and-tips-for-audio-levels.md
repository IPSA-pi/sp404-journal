## Question
Are there good videos about audio levels, levelling samples and mixing properly?

## Key takeaways
- NORMALIZE: `[START/END]`, select the pad, press `[VALUE]` > NORMALIZE. It rewrites the sample and can't be undone. ([Processing a sample](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7910874778476427.html))
- Leave headroom before effects: drums peaking around -12 to -9 dBFS, the kick as the anchor, about -6 dB spare if you will mix later.
- The Compressor effect has no threshold knob, only SUSTAIN, ATTACK, RATIO and LEVEL, so how hard it works depends on how loud the samples are going in. Its manual note warns it can output a very loud sound. ([Compressor](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7996276378576267.html))
- Suggested learning order from one guide: Ricky Tinez, then STLNDRMS, then spvidz.
