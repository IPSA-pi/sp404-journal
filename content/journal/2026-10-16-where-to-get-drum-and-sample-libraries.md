## Question
Where can I get drum and sample libraries for the SP-404MKII?

## Key takeaways
- The manuals list no sample sources; everything below is general knowledge, and offers and prices were not checked.
- Free: Roland's SP-404MKII downloads page (factory content and occasional sound packs), Looperman, Freesound, label freebies (Splice, Loopmasters, Cymatics, MSXII) and kits shared on r/SP404.
- Paid: Splice Sounds (per-sound subscription), Loopmasters/Loopcloud, Native Instruments, Output Arcade, and SP-specific sellers such as MSXII Sound Design, The Drum Broker and Sample Magic.
- Classic drum-machine sets (808, 909, SP-1200, MPC60) suit boom-bap and lo-fi styles.
- Make your own: sample vinyl or any other source through the unit's inputs, or chop public-domain recordings from archive.org. ([Sampling (SAMPLING)](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7903834778468747.html))
- Whatever the source, the unit imports only WAV, AIFF and MP3; FLAC and M4A need the SP-404MK2 App. ([Main specifications](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/8010023578592395.html))
- Keep folder and file names short, because you browse them on the small display.

## Details
**Free**
- Roland: the SP-404MKII downloads page (https://www.roland.com/global/products/sp-404mk2/downloads/) has the factory content, useful to restore the original samples. Roland has also given away extra SP-404MKII sound packs through Roland Cloud and its "Roland Sound" pages over time.
- Looperman and Freesound: large community libraries. Licences vary, so check each file before releasing music with it.
- Producer and label freebies: Splice, Loopmasters, Cymatics, MSXII and others regularly give away free packs, usually in exchange for an email signup.
- Reddit r/SP404: people share their own kits there, often already trimmed for the 404.

**Paid / subscription**
- Splice Sounds: a subscription where you download single sounds, so you only take the hits you want.
- Loopmasters/Loopcloud, Native Instruments, Output Arcade: large catalogues sold as packs or subscriptions.
- SP-specific sellers: MSXII Sound Design, The Drum Broker, Sample Magic and several YouTube SP-404 creators sell packs made for the unit, sometimes including ready-made project files.
- Drum-machine sample sets: packs of classic machines like the 808, 909, SP-1200 and MPC60 suit boom-bap and lo-fi styles well.

**Make your own**
- Resample vinyl, YouTube or other recordings straight into the unit through the inputs (the manual's Sampling section covers recording from the inputs).
- Old public-domain recordings on archive.org are a legal source of material to chop.

**Getting the samples onto the unit.** Use WAV, AIFF or MP3. FLAC and M4A only work through the SP-404MK2 App. Keep the folder and file names short, because you browse them on the small display. Once a pack is downloaded, Claude can convert anything that isn't WAV, AIFF or MP3, remove the extra files, and copy it to `F:\IMPORT\` with sensible folder names.

**Note from the manual.** The manual itself doesn't mention the downloads page. Its FACTORY RESET > ALL DATA restores the samples and patterns to the factory defaults from the unit's own storage, so the download is only needed if that data is lost or you want the files on a computer.
