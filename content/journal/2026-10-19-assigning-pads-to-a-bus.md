## Question
How can I assign a pad to a bus?

## Key takeaways
- Press `[BUS FX]` to choose BUS 1 or BUS 2, then hold `[REMAIN]` and press a pad: it toggles between that bus and DRY. ([Assigning each sample to a bus](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900225178464907.html))
- To move a pad from BUS 1 to BUS 2, select BUS 2 with `[BUS FX]` first, then `[REMAIN]` + pad. ([Assigning each sample to a bus](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900225178464907.html))
- `[REMAIN]` + `[EXT SOURCE]` sets the bus for INPUT and LINE IN; `[EXT SOURCE]` then shows lit = BUS 1, blinking = BUS 2, dark = DRY. ([Assigning each sample to a bus](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900225178464907.html))
- BUS 1/2 feed BUS 3/4; EFX SET > OTHER > DRY Routing puts DRY after BUS 4 ("DRY") or before BUS 3 ("BUS 3"). ([Configuring the bus routing](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7954343578531723.html))

## Details
While `[REMAIN]` is held the pads show their bus: orange BUS 1, green BUS 2, white
DRY. The colours can be changed under UTILITY > PAD SET (BUS COLOR). `[REMAIN]` + a
bank button sets the bus for the whole bank at once.
