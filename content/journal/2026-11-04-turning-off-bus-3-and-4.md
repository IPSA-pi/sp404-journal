## Question
How can I mute BUS 3 and BUS 4?

## Key takeaways
- Hold `[VALUE]` and press `[BUS FX]` to temporarily bypass the BUS 3 and BUS 4 effects; the FAVORITE setting is unchanged. ([Using effects with BUS 3, BUS 4 (FAVORITE)](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7954932378532491.html))
- To switch them off in the settings: `[SHIFT]` + pad `[16]`, `[CTRL 3]` to FAVORITE, `[VALUE]` to "Bypass", `[EXIT]`. Choose FAVORITE 1–16 to turn them back on. ([Using effects with BUS 3, BUS 4 (FAVORITE)](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7954932378532491.html))
- Both only bypass the effects; the audio keeps playing. The manual has no way to silence BUS 3/4 themselves.

## Details
BUS 1 and BUS 2 feed BUS 3 and BUS 4, which act as master effects, so there is
nothing to "mute" there beyond the effects. MUTE BUS (`[SHIFT]` + `[BUS FX]`)
applies to BUS 1/2, not BUS 3/4.
