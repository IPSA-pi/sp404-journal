## Question
How can I change the delay button's effect from Sync Delay to tape delay?

## Key takeaways
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[16]` opens EFX SET; turn `[CTRL 3]` to DIRECT. ([DIRECT FX](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7956468378534795.html))
- Press `[DELAY]` (or select Direct FX3 with `[VALUE]`), press `[VALUE]`, turn to "Tape Echo", press `[VALUE]`, then `[EXIT]`. ([DIRECT FX](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7956468378534795.html))
- The slots are `[FILTER+DRIVE]` FX1, `[RESONATOR]` FX2, `[DELAY]` FX3, `[ISOLATOR]` FX4, `[DJFX LOOPER]` FX5. ([DIRECT FX](https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7956468378534795.html))

## Details
Tape Echo is the RE-201 Space Echo simulation. Sync Delay goes back the same way, and
any other button can take a different effect.
