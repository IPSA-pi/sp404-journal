## Playback modes
- `[GATE]` on: the sample plays only while the pad is held. Off: each press starts playback. `[SHIFT]` + `[GATE]` switches GATE for every sample in the bank.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-chopping-samples-and-sample-tips, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7887245978451083.html, status: manual -->
- One-shot playback: hold `[VALUE]` and press `[GATE]` (`[GATE]` blinks slowly); the sample always plays to the end and turns LOOP off.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-chopping-samples-and-sample-tips, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7887911578451851.html, status: manual -->
- `[LOOP]` makes a sample repeat; each pad press starts or stops it. `[SHIFT]` + `[LOOP]` plays it forwards then backwards (ping-pong).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-11, entry: 2026-10-11-recording-a-loop-in-sync-with-project-bpm, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7888500378452619.html, status: manual -->
- Loops usually want LOOP and BPM SYNC on; chops usually want GATE off.

## Editing several pads at once
- Holding `[REMAIN]` while changing a parameter on the pitch/speed or envelope screen changes it for every sample in the bank; holding `[COPY]` changes it for the samples in the same mute group.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-chopping-samples-and-sample-tips, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/8004340378585483.html, status: manual -->
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[8]` opens MUTE GROUP: pick a group (A–J) with `[VALUE]` and press pads to add them; only the last-played pad in a group sounds, which stops slices of one loop overlapping.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-chopping-samples-and-sample-tips, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7895796378459531.html, status: manual -->

## Pad parameters
- Hold `[REMAIN]` to show the parameters of the selected pad's sample (and the remaining time while it plays).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-chopping-samples-and-sample-tips, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7915277978480267.html, status: manual -->
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[6]` (INIT PARAM) resets a sample's parameters (start/end points, tempo and so on) to defaults, after a confirmation.
