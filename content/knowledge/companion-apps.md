## SP-404MK2 App
- The SP-404MK2 App can import FLAC and M4A as well as WAV, AIFF and MP3; the unit itself (via the SD card) takes only WAV, AIFF and MP3.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-11, entry: 2026-10-11-ableton-latin-percussion-pack-to-sd-card, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/8010023578592395.html, status: manual -->
- The app connects over USB: connect the cable, power the unit on, launch the app and click Connect; the Samples screen appears. Never power off or unplug while the app shows "Working...".
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-18, entry: 2026-10-18-using-the-sp-404mkii-with-ableton-live, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_app_v4/en-US/8017908378641291.html, status: manual -->
- Import by dragging files (one or several) from the computer onto empty pads in the app; dropping onto a used pad overwrites it, and files shorter than 100 ms may fail.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-18, entry: 2026-10-18-using-the-sp-404mkii-with-ableton-live, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_app_v4/en-US/8022260378646667.html, status: manual -->
- The app's TS/PS tab sets a sample's BPM (40.0–200.0), has "Analyze BPM" and "Set BPM by St/End" (tempo from the playback range), a BPM Sync checkbox, Time Stretch 50.0–150.0, Pitch Coarse (-12–12) and Fine (-100–100), and a Vinyl mode that links speed and pitch.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-18, entry: 2026-10-18-using-the-sp-404mkii-with-ableton-live, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_app_v4/en-US/8021236378645131.html, status: manual -->
- "Set BPM by St/End" is the quickest way to give an exact-bar DAW export its tempo.

## Melodics
- Melodics is a finger-drumming lesson app. Connect over USB and launch it; the unit enters Melodics controller mode. If it isn't detected, choose ROLAND SP-404MKII under Settings > Connected Instruments.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-28, entry: 2026-10-28-melodics-koala-and-variphrase, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_v300_melodics/eng/98337136.html, status: manual -->
- In Melodics mode `[ROLL]` starts a lesson, `[PITCH/SPEED]` switches performance/practice, `[CTRL 1]`–`[CTRL 3]` set computer audio, metronome and YOUR NOTES volume, `[SHIFT]` + `[EXIT]` leaves.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-28, entry: 2026-10-28-melodics-koala-and-variphrase, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_v300_melodics/eng/98337134.html, status: manual -->

## Koala Sampler
- Koala Sampler (iOS/Android) puts the unit in Koala Controller Mode over USB, with Koala 1.4082 or later; Lightning iPhones need Apple's camera adapter.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-28, entry: 2026-10-28-melodics-koala-and-variphrase, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_v4_koala_sampler/eng/164266034.html, status: manual -->
- In Koala mode banks A–D control Koala's banks and E–J remain the SP's own sampler. `[EXT SOURCE]` brings in the phone's audio; `[SHIFT]` + `[EXIT]` returns to normal, `[SHIFT]` + `[MARK]` reconnects.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-28, entry: 2026-10-28-melodics-koala-and-variphrase, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_v4_koala_sampler/eng/164266033.html, status: manual -->
