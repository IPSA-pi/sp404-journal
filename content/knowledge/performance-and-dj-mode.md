## DJ mode
- Press bank `[D/I]` and `[E/J]` together to enter DJ mode. `[VALUE]` > CH1 SELECT or CH2 SELECT, press a pad, then ENTER assigns a sample to each channel.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- `[CTRL 1]` and `[CTRL 2]` are the CH1 and CH2 levels. `[CTRL 3]` is CUE MIX or X-FADE (crossfader); `[START/END]` switches between them.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- DJ pads: `[13]`/`[15]` play/pause, `[9]`/`[11]` back to the start, `[14]`/`[16]` send to CUE, `[10]`/`[12]` SYNC (pad `[10]` makes CH1 follow CH2).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- Tempo in DJ mode: BPM+ on pads `[2]`/`[4]`, BPM- on `[6]`/`[8]`, BEND+ on `[1]`/`[3]`, BEND- on `[5]`/`[7]`; `[DEL]` + a BPM pad resets the tempo.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- `[ROLL]` + pad `[13]` (CH1) or `[15]` (CH2) rolls a deck; `[SHIFT]` + `[ROLL]` sets ROLL SIZE to 1/4, 1/2, 1 or 2 measures before rolling. The manual has no "Quick Loop".
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- `[REMAIN]` + pad `[14]` (CH1) or `[16]` (CH2) cycles that deck through BUS-1, BUS-2 and DRY, so each deck can have its own effects.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- `[RESAMPLE]` + `[VALUE]` picks the fader curve for the levels and crossfader: FAST CUT, LINEAR, SQUARE, CUBIC.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7937626778510219.html, status: manual -->
- `[HOLD]` switches DJ mode to PAD MODE so the pads play samples again; pads blinking orange are on a deck and can't be played.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7938599578510987.html, status: manual -->
- Patterns in DJ mode: `[PATTERN SELECT]`, pick the pattern, `[PATTERN SELECT]`, `[PATTERN EDIT]`, then pad `[13]` plays it.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7939111578511755.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > DJ Mode TS type sets how tempo changes sound in DJ mode: VINYL (pitch follows speed), BACKING or ENSEMBLE (independent).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Prepare DJ tracks as 48 kHz / 16-bit WAVs with intros trimmed, keep one project per set, and set each track's BPM by hand (MANU) when AUTO detection is off, since SYNC depends on it.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, status: unverified -->

## Headphone cue
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[3]` opens CUE; `[CTRL 3]` balances "C" (the cued sample) against "M" (the main output). Cued audio goes only to PHONES, one sample at a time.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7942721178516363.html, status: manual -->

## Live mode and power
- LIVE MODE: `[SHIFT]` + hold `[REMAIN]` for three seconds. It disables `[DEL]`, `[REC]`, `[RESAMPLE]`, `[COPY]` and `[MARK]`; do the same to turn it off.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7897639578461835.html, status: manual -->
- Six AA batteries last about 3.5 hours (Ni-MH, 1,900 mAh) or 2.5 hours (alkaline).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-17, entry: 2026-10-17-dj-mode-as-a-standalone-mixer, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7876327578438795.html, status: manual -->

## Moves during a performance
- While a pattern plays, hold `[SUB PAD]` and press another pattern pad to switch immediately; a plain press queues it for when the current one ends.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7919732378487179.html, status: manual -->
- Pad mute: `[SHIFT]` + `[REVERSE]` + pad (muted pads light red). `[SHIFT]` + `[REVERSE]` + `[REMAIN]` latches mute mode until `[EXIT]`. Pads showing a pattern (purple) can't be muted.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7894388378457995.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > Pad MUTE = "Master" keeps muted pads audible in the headphones, without effects.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7894388378457995.html, status: manual -->
- Pause all samples with `[SHIFT]` + `[HOLD]`; press it again to resume.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7897050778461067.html, status: manual -->
- Stop everything with `[EXIT]` pressed quickly four times, or `[SHIFT]` + `[EXIT]`.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-25, entry: 2026-10-25-recording-guitar-over-a-pattern, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7896385178460299.html, status: manual -->
- Rolls: hold `[ROLL]` and press up to four pads. `[SHIFT]` + `[ROLL]` steps the interval from 1/4 to 1/64, then triplets 3/8 to 3/64.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7890497178454923.html, status: manual -->
- During a roll, hold `[ROLL]` and turn `[VALUE]` clockwise to speed it up; it can't go slower than where it started.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7890497178454923.html, status: manual -->
- `[REVERSE]` while a sample plays flips it. SYSTEM > Reverse Type "404" restarts from the end point, "303" reverses from the current position.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7889089178453387.html, status: manual -->
- PAD LINK GROUPS (`[SHIFT]` + pad `[7]`): up to four pads per group, groups A–J; pressing one pad plays them all, which starts layered loops together.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-27, entry: 2026-10-27-polymeter-and-polyrhythm-with-loops, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7895130778458763.html, status: manual -->
- Keep the main loop on a bus with a filter or DJFX Looper and ride the knobs for transitions; keep the other bus for throw effects like delay or reverb.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-24, entry: 2026-10-24-silent-pad-select-and-live-performance-tricks, status: unverified -->
