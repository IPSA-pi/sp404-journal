## Bus FX basics
- The six effect buttons and `[CTRL 1]`–`[CTRL 3]` control BUS 1 and BUS 2. `[BUS FX]` picks the bus: lit orange = BUS 1, blinking orange = BUS 2.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7899047578463371.html, status: manual -->
- `[FILTER+DRIVE]`, `[RESONATOR]`, `[DELAY]`, `[ISOLATOR]`, `[DJFX LOOPER]` and `[MFX]` toggle an effect on the selected bus; pressing a lit or blinking button turns it off.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7899047578463371.html, status: manual -->
- `[CTRL 1]`–`[CTRL 3]` edit the main parameters; hold `[VALUE]` and turn them for the sub-parameters.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7899047578463371.html, status: manual -->
- Each bus runs one effect at a time; setting the same effect on both buses shows "Same EFX on other BUS".
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7899047578463371.html, status: manual -->
- Effect grab: hold `[VALUE]` and press an effect button; the effect is on only while the button is held.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7902733978467211.html, status: manual -->
- `[REMAIN]` + an effect button keeps the effect edit screen from timing out; `[VALUE]` flips between main and sub-parameters, `[EXIT]` or `[REMAIN]` leaves.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7901249178466443.html, status: manual -->

## Saving and shared parameters
- The main parameters of BUS 1 and BUS 2 are kept after power-off only if you hold `[SHIFT]` and hold `[MARK]` for at least three seconds; sub-parameters are kept automatically.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7901249178466443.html, status: manual -->
- Sub-parameters are shared between INPUT FX, BUS 1 and BUS 2 (and between BUS 3 and BUS 4): the same effect in two places shares its settings.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7901249178466443.html, status: manual -->

## Choosing effects (MFX and DIRECT FX)
- Hold `[MFX]` and turn `[VALUE]` or `[CTRL 3]` (or press pads `[1]`–`[16]`) to choose any effect, then release. `[MFX]` + `[DJFX LOOPER]` reaches #17 and up, `[MFX]` + `[ISOLATOR]` #33 and up.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900737178465675.html, status: manual -->
- DIRECT FX changes what the five effect buttons hold: `[SHIFT]` + pad `[13]` > EFX SET > DIRECT (Direct FX1–5 = `[FILTER+DRIVE]` … `[DJFX LOOPER]`).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7956468378534795.html, status: manual -->

## Sending pads to buses
- Hold `[REMAIN]`: pads show their bus (factory colours orange = BUS 1, green = BUS 2, white = DRY). Pressing a pad while holding `[REMAIN]` toggles it between the bus selected with `[BUS FX]` and DRY (no BUS 1/2 effects).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900225178464907.html, status: manual -->
- `[REMAIN]` + a bank button sets the bus for a whole bank; `[REMAIN]` + `[EXT SOURCE]` sets it for the external inputs.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7900225178464907.html, status: manual -->

## Delay and keeping tails
- There is no tails/spillover setting in the manual: switching a delay off with its button cuts the repeats.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: unverified -->
- Ko-Da-Ma has TIME, FEEDBACK and SEND ("the volume of sound sent to the effect"), plus L/H DAMP F and MODE (SINGLE/PAN). Sync Delay, Tape Echo and TimeCtrlDly have LEVEL (the volume of the effect sound) instead of SEND.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7978791578554763.html, status: manual -->
- To keep a delay tail, turn Ko-Da-Ma's SEND down instead of switching the effect off, so existing repeats fade at the FEEDBACK setting. Assign Ko-Da-Ma to `[DELAY]` with DIRECT FX for one-button access.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: unverified -->
- Another way to keep a tail: leave the delay on and move pads to DRY with `[REMAIN]` + pad, so they stop feeding it.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: unverified -->
- FEEDBACK close to 99% can build into runaway feedback.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, status: unverified -->

## MUTE BUS
- `[SHIFT]` + `[BUS FX]` (MUTE BUS) mutes the input to the bus so only the reverb or delay tail is heard; pressing it again lets the sound back in and turns the bus effect off at the same time.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7903245978467979.html, status: manual -->
- EFX SET > OTHER > Mute Bus sets its scope: ALL (BUS 1 and BUS 2) or BUS (only the bus selected with `[BUS FX]`).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7971969178547083.html, status: manual -->

## Routing, BUS 3/4 and side chain
- EFX SET opens with `[SHIFT]` + pad `[16]` (or `[SHIFT]` + pad `[13]` > EFX SET); `[CTRL 3]` picks the tab (FAVORITE, BUS 3, BUS 4, SIDE CHAIN, DIRECT, OTHER).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7953677978530955.html, status: manual -->
- On the FAVORITE tab, pressing `[VALUE]` switches BUS 1/2 routing between TYPE A (serial, BUS 1 into BUS 2) and TYPE B (parallel).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7954343578531723.html, status: manual -->
- BUS 3 and BUS 4 sit after BUS 1/2 as master effects; their effects are stored in FAVORITE 1–16 (Bypass disables them), independent of the project. `[VALUE]` + `[BUS FX]` bypasses them without changing the FAVORITE.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7954932378532491.html, status: manual -->
- EFX SET > OTHER > DRY Routing = "BUS3" sends the DRY bus through BUS 3 and BUS 4; "DRY" keeps it effect-free.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7971969178547083.html, status: manual -->
- SIDE CHAIN: SOURCE is the bus that triggers compression (e.g. the kick's bus), TARGET the bus that ducks; adjust THRESHOLD, RATIO, RELEASE and GAIN.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/564410379565838475.html, status: manual -->

## Input FX
- Input FX (EFX SET > OTHER > Input FX) process INPUT and LINE IN; Auto Pitch, Vocoder, Harmony and Gt Amp Sim are available only there. Input Bus sends the input to DRY, BUS 1 or BUS 2.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7957057178535563.html, status: manual -->

## Effect parameters
- Filter+Drive: CUTOFF, RESONANCE, DRIVE; sub FLT TYPE (HPF/LPF), LOW FREQ, LOW GAIN.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7974004378548619.html, status: manual -->
- Resonator: ROOT, BRIGHT, FEEDBACK; sub CHORD, PANNING, ENV MOD.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7974593178549387.html, status: manual -->
- Sync Delay: TIME (1/32–1/1, including triplets and dotted), FEEDBACK (0–99%), LEVEL; sub L DAMP F, H DAMP F (cut lows/highs on each repeat).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7975258778550155.html, status: manual -->
- Isolator: LOW, MID, HIGH, each -INF or -41.87 to +12 dB, so a band can be cut completely.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7975770778550923.html, status: manual -->
- DJFX Looper: LENGTH, SPEED (-100 to 100: negative plays backwards, 0 stops), LOOP SW.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7976359578551691.html, status: manual -->
- Tape Echo: TIME (10–800 ms), FEEDBACK, LEVEL; sub MODE (playback head combinations), W/F RATE, W/F DEPTH.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7981812378558603.html, status: manual -->
- Stopper slows playback like a turntable stopping. Back Spin needs the sample to play (charge) for up to about three seconds first; BACK SW blinks while charging.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7999015578579339.html, status: manual -->
- Scatter's SCATTER parameter switches the effect on/off; it rearranges a loop in steps for a glitchy, digital groove.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-14, entry: 2026-10-14-effects-buttons-and-delay-feedback, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7977025178552459.html, status: manual -->
