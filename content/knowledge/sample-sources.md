## Free sources
- Roland's SP-404MKII downloads page (https://www.roland.com/global/products/sp-404mk2/downloads/) has the factory content, and Roland has given away extra sound packs through Roland Cloud and its "Roland Sound" pages over time.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->
- Looperman and Freesound are large community libraries; licences vary per file, so check before releasing music with them.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->
- Splice, Loopmasters, Cymatics, MSXII and other labels regularly give away free packs, usually for an email signup.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->
- Reddit r/SP404 members share their own kits, often already trimmed for the 404.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->

## Paid and subscription
- Splice Sounds is a per-sound subscription, so you only download the hits you want; Loopmasters/Loopcloud, Native Instruments and Output Arcade sell large catalogues as packs or subscriptions.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->
- SP-specific sellers (MSXII Sound Design, The Drum Broker, Sample Magic, several YouTube SP-404 creators) sell packs made for the unit, sometimes with ready-made project files.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->
- Classic drum-machine sets (808, 909, SP-1200, MPC60) suit boom-bap and lo-fi styles well.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->

## Make your own
- Sample vinyl, YouTube or any other recording straight into the unit through its inputs (SAMPLING records the sound inputted into the unit).
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7903834778468747.html, status: manual -->
- Public-domain recordings on archive.org are a legal source of material to chop.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, status: unverified -->

## Getting packs onto the unit
- Downloaded packs must end up as WAV, AIFF or MP3 (FLAC and M4A only via the SP-404MK2 App); see the SD card topic for conversion and import steps.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-16, entry: 2026-10-16-where-to-get-drum-and-sample-libraries, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/8010023578592395.html, status: manual -->
- UTILITY > FACTORY RESET > ALL DATA restores the samples and patterns to the factory defaults from the unit itself, so the Roland download is only needed if that data is lost or you want the files on a computer.
