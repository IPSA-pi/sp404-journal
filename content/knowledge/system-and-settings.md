## UTILITY and SYSTEM
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[13]` opens the UTILITY MENU; SYSTEM settings are in tabs chosen with `[CTRL 3]`, edited with `[VALUE]`.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-usb-c-recording-and-project-bpm, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7952346778529419.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > FileSystem: "Latin1" recognises letters, numbers and symbols only, so files with double-byte characters in their names can't be recognised; "Multi-Byte" recognises them but shows them garbled. Restart the unit after changing it.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-bandcamp-albums-unsupported-file, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > GENERAL > MARK Function chooses what `[MARK]` does: "SBS Def" (skip-back, 25 s), "SBS Long" (40 s) or "Looper".
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-usb-c-recording-and-project-bpm, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > App Auto Connection ON connects automatically when Serato DJ, Koala Sampler or Melodics starts.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-28, entry: 2026-10-28-melodics-koala-and-variphrase, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->

- SYSTEM > GENERAL > EFX Knob Mode: "Catch" changes a value only once the knob reaches it, "Direct" jumps to the knob, "Manual" applies the knob positions when you switch effects.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-knob-catch-mode, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Edit Knob Mode (Catch or Direct) does the same for the editing screens; DJ mode always works in Catch.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-knob-catch-mode, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Load Project: "Last" opens the last-used project at power-on; 1–16 always opens that project.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Sub Pad Mode: `[SUB PAD]` retriggers the current pad (Retrig) or switches to skip-back mode (SkipBack).
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- PTN Change Mode: samples stop when the pattern changes (MKII) or keep playing (SX).
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Bank Mute ON mutes the sound automatically when a sample in another bank plays.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- Pop-up Time (Normal, Short, OFF) sets how long pop-ups show; Start-up Screen "Skip" skips some startup screens.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- GAIN > Noise Gate (OFF, -9, -12, -18 dB, -Inf) reduces the noise floor on LINE IN and INPUT.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->

## PAD SET
- TRIGGER > Curve Type: Lin (natural), Exp (hard hits change more), Log (soft hits change more), Fix (always 127).
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7971073178546315.html, status: manual -->
- TRIGGER > Threshold (1–100) ignores hits below a minimum force, e.g. vibration from other pads; Trig Span (1–10) higher guards against accidental double hits.
  <!-- learned: 2026-11-04, entry: 2026-11-04-useful-utility-settings, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7971073178546315.html, status: manual -->

## Metronome and count-in
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[9]` only arms the metronome. It sounds while sampling or resampling (CLICK > Metronome:REC) or recording a pattern (Metronome:PTN), not during plain playback.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-27, entry: 2026-10-27-why-the-metronome-is-silent, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > CLICK: Click Level 1–5, and Output Assign ON also sends the click to LINE OUT and USB.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-27, entry: 2026-10-27-why-the-metronome-is-silent, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- To practise against the click without recording, start pattern recording and press `[REC]` again for rehearsal mode.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-27, entry: 2026-10-27-why-the-metronome-is-silent, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7920321178487947.html, status: manual -->
- CLICK > Count-In:REC and Count-In:PTN each take OFF, 1 MEAS, 2 MEAS or WAIT; `[SHIFT]` + pad `[10]` cycles them.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-27, entry: 2026-10-27-why-the-metronome-is-silent, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
