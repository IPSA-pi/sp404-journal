## UTILITY and SYSTEM
- `[SHIFT]` + pad `[13]` opens the UTILITY MENU; SYSTEM settings are in tabs chosen with `[CTRL 3]`, edited with `[VALUE]`.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-usb-c-recording-and-project-bpm, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7952346778529419.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > FileSystem: "Latin1" recognises letters, numbers and symbols only, so files with double-byte characters in their names can't be recognised; "Multi-Byte" recognises them but shows them garbled. Restart the unit after changing it.
  <!-- learned: 2026-10-12, entry: 2026-10-12-bandcamp-albums-unsupported-file, manual: https://static.roland.com/manuals/sp-404mk2_reference_v550/en-US/7964634778545547.html, status: manual -->
- SYSTEM > GENERAL > MARK Function chooses what `[MARK]` does: "SBS Def" (skip-back, 25 s), "SBS Long" (40 s) or "Looper".
